CarryHub Digital Commerce Platform

A modern e-commerce platform developed to help retailers establish a professional online presence, showcase products, streamline online shopping, and simulate secure digital payments using the PayPal Sandbox environment.

Project Overview

The retail industry is rapidly shifting toward digital commerce, where customers expect to browse products, compare options, and complete purchases from anywhere at any time.

CarryHub was developed by MLUE Technology as a modern digital commerce platform that enables retailers to showcase products professionally while providing customers with an intuitive and engaging online shopping experience. The platform demonstrates how businesses can transition from traditional retail operations to digital commerce through responsive web technologies, secure payment workflows, and scalable system architecture.

The Business Challenge

Many small and medium-sized retailers face challenges when trying to establish an effective online sales channel. Common challenges include:

  • Limited online visibility for products.
  • Dependence on physical stores for sales.
  • Manual order processing.
  • Difficulty managing digital customer interactions.
  • Lack of secure online payment workflows.
  • Inconsistent shopping experiences across devices.

Without a reliable e-commerce platform, businesses may struggle to expand their market reach and compete in today's digital economy.

Our Solution

MLUE Technology designed and developed the CarryHub Digital Commerce Platform to provide retailers with a complete online shopping solution. The platform allows customers to browse products, search and filter items, view detailed product information, manage shopping carts, and complete a simulated checkout process using the PayPal Sandbox environment.

The solution was built using a scalable server-side architecture that can be extended to support production payment gateways, inventory integration, order management, customer accounts, and business analytics.

Solution Highlights

Modern Landing Experience

Introduce customers to CarryHub through a clean homepage featuring highlighted products, intuitive navigation, and a professional retail interface.

Secure Customer Access

User registration and login functionality provide personalized shopping experiences while protecting customer information.

Product Information

Each product page provides detailed information, pricing, descriptions, and high-quality images to support informed purchasing decisions.

Cart Management

Customers can add, remove, and review products before proceeding to checkout, creating a smooth and user-friendly shopping experience.

Checkout Workflow

A streamlined checkout process guides customers from cart review to payment confirmation through an intuitive purchasing workflow.

Payment Simulation

Demonstrates secure online payment processing using the PayPal Sandbox environment, allowing safe testing of digital payment workflows.

Customer Order Management

Allows customers to track their orders, view order history, and manage their shopping experience with ease.

Admin Order Management

Provides administrators with tools to monitor, update, and manage customer orders efficiently.

Technologies Used

Node.js Express.js EJS PostgreSQL PayPal Sandbox HTML5 CSS3 JavaScript

Development Highlights

  • Server-Side Rendering: Dynamic pages generated using EJS templates for improved performance and efficient content rendering.
  • Secure Authentication: User authentication and session management designed to protect customer accounts and shopping activities.
  • Digital Payment Workflow: Integrated PayPal Sandbox to simulate secure online payment processing during development and testing.
  • Responsive Design: Mobile-first interface delivering a seamless shopping experience across multiple screen sizes.
  • Scalable Architecture: Application structure designed to support future enhancements including production payment gateways, inventory synchronization, and order tracking.
